# Offers 23: one line, four shapes, three endings

## Shared film: 23A, 23B and 23C are identical up to the last stop

There is one barbed wire, never cut. The Bridge's sentence condenses into it, and as the film plays the same wire draws one recognisable shape for each service, hanging from the line:

| Service | Shape | Why |
|---|---|---|

| Photography | a circle around the red point | a lens with its subject in it |
| Rebuilds | a triangle that arrives crooked and is pulled true | structure, fixed honestly |
| Care | an infinity loop threaded on the line | launch day is not goodbye |

At each stop the shape being discussed is lit and the rest of the wire waits in the dark (the "story light"). The paragraph arrives once the wire is still and leaves before it moves again.

## The three endings (8.50 → 9.40)

- **23A, The Line.** The camera comes down to eye level. Because every shape lies flat on the line's plane, they fold edge-on, one after another, into a single unbroken barbed wire across the screen: the hero's snapped wire, mended, with the red point resting on it. The line runs off both edges, ready to become the rail that Packages sits on.
- **23B, Three Lines.** The shapes are pulled taut. The wire lays itself back and forth into three parallel lines across the frame, one per package, so the next section can hang a tier on each without drawing any boxes.
- **23C, The Full Stop.** The barbs fold away and the wire winds tightly around the red point into a steel ring. Then the full stop opens into a red field that fills the screen. "Tell us what you need" sits on the red, and Packages can begin on that field.

All three keep the approved Hero, About and Bridge byte for byte. The type is always flat, and the wire is the only 3D object.
