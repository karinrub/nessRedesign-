# Offers 22, "Point of View": the ending first

## The final shot

One barbed-wire sculpture, made of four pieces floating at different depths in the dark and joined by straight steel lines. From anywhere in the room it is four unrelated forms. From one exact spot, the point of view, all four line up into a single ring around a red point: the full stop of "Tell us what you need". The wire's two cut ends meet at the top of the ring, and only from there do they look joined.

- **Meaning.** The Bridge says people meet a business in pieces, before they ever meet you. A customer sees you from one point of view. NESS finds that point, where the pieces read as one clear mark.
- **The pieces are the services.** Each is a quarter of the ring, pushed to its own depth and shaped into its own 3D form.
- **The connecting lines run along sight-lines.** Every straight steel line between two pieces points at the point of view. From the side they look like perspective lines converging on an empty spot in space. That spot is where the film ends, so the wire was always pointing at the ending.

## The three final forms I considered

1. **Lobed form, after Ruth Asawa.** A hanging column of four wound lobes, each a service. Beautiful, but it is a tower again, its interiors all read as tunnels, and the reveal would only be a pull-back.
2. **One long gesture, after Calder.** A single sweeping line in space with four gestures along it. The reveal would be an orbit, which is generic, and the line has no inner logic.
3. **Point of View.** Anamorphosis, as in Felice Varini, Michael Murphy and Markus Raetz: chaos everywhere, clarity from one spot. It is the only one where the ending explains every earlier move, and it ties back to the Bridge's sentence and its full stop. **Chosen.**

## Rules

- **Type:** all flat and always square to the screen. Where it sits in space, the wire can pass in front of it or behind it, so it is woven rather than layered.
- **Stops:** the wire moves only between stops; paragraphs arrive at a stop and leave before the next move.
- **Red:** only the point at the centre.
- **Layout:** desktop, 1440×900. The film gains one stop (q = 9.4, p = 14.2); the scroll length becomes 9.7.
